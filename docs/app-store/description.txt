Capture your screen with a compact toolbar that stays out of your way.

Easyshot opens when you need it, remembers your last capture mode and area, and quits when your session ends. Your screenshots save as PNG files and copy to the clipboard by default.

CHOOSE YOUR CAPTURE
Capture a full display, point at a window, capture the active window, draw an area, or reuse your last rectangle. Switch modes with keys 1–5. Move or resize the selection, draw a fresh area, and optionally capture as soon as you finish drawing.

SAVE AND SHARE
Choose a local capture folder, use the clipboard, and enable a brief thumbnail preview. Rapid captures use unique filenames. Your library keeps screenshots and recordings together.

EDIT YOUR CAPTURES
Add arrows, text, shapes, highlights and numbered steps. Blur, pixelate or redact details. Crop, resize, rotate, combine images, and save editable projects.

CAPTURE MORE
Record your screen, window or area as MP4, with optional system audio, microphone and webcam. Trim recordings and export GIFs. Capture long content by scrolling manually and adding overlapping frames. Recognize text and QR codes on your Mac.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, advertising, analytics or subscription. Your captures and recognized text stay on your Mac unless you choose to share them. macOS screen, camera and microphone permissions stay under your control.

Designed for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 26 or later, with native Liquid Glass controls.
