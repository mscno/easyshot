Easyshot Support

Easyshot requires an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 26 or later.

Getting started
Open Easyshot to show the capture toolbar. Select a mode with the icons or keys 1–5. Move or resize your rectangle, or draw a new one. Press Return or Capture to take the screenshot; Escape cancels. With Snap on draw enabled, a completed new rectangle captures automatically.

Permissions
Allow Screen Recording when macOS asks. If access was denied, enable Easyshot in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording, then reopen the app. Camera and microphone permissions are optional and used only for recording features you enable.

Saving files
In the Mac App Store edition, choose a screenshots folder in Settings or when prompted on your first capture. The macOS folder picker grants permission to save there. If a folder moves, disappears or becomes unavailable, choose it again. Captures copy to the clipboard and save to disk by default. Settings lets you change both behaviors.

Capture modes
1: Full Screen. 2: Window under the pointer. 3: Area. 4: Active Window. 5: Last Area. D or N draws a new area. The last mode is remembered.

Long screenshots
The Mac App Store edition uses manual scrolling: scroll the content yourself, then choose Add Frame. Include overlapping content between frames so Easyshot can stitch them.

Recording
A visible recording bar remains while recording. Stop recording there. Screen, microphone, system audio and webcam settings control what is included.

Privacy
Easyshot does not send captures or analytics to Paraply. Sharing, clipboard access and cloud-synced folders are controlled by the apps and services you choose. Removing a capture from history keeps its file on disk.

Reporting a problem
Include your macOS version, Easyshot version, capture mode and steps to reproduce. Share a screenshot or recording only if you are comfortable sending its contents. Never send credentials or private screenshots unnecessarily.
